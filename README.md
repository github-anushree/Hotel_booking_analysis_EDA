# 🏨 Hotel Booking Analysis – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on performing an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
on hotel booking data to understand booking patterns, cancellation behavior,
pricing trends, and seasonality. The dataset contains booking information for
City Hotels and Resort Hotels over three years (2015–2017).

The objective of this analysis is to identify key factors that influence booking
cancellations and revenue, and to provide actionable business insights that can
help hotels improve operational efficiency and decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
- **Rows:** 119,390  
- **Columns:** 32  
- **Hotels:** City Hotel & Resort Hotel  
- **Time Period:** 2015–2017

## 🔍 Key Steps Performed

### 1. Data Understanding & Cleaning
- Checked data quality, missing values, duplicates, and data types
- Handled missing values using business logic
- Created new features such as `total_stay_nights` and `total_guests`

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
- Univariate analysis to understand individual variable distributions
- Bivariate analysis to study relationships with booking cancellation
- Time and seasonality analysis to identify monthly and yearly trends

### 3. Business Insights
- Identified major drivers of booking cancellations
- Analyzed pricing behavior using Average Daily Rate (ADR)
- Compared performance of City Hotels vs Resort Hotels
- Studied customer behavior, booking channels, and seasonal demand

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- City Hotels receive higher booking demand compared to Resort Hotels
- Over 27% of total bookings were canceled, posing a revenue risk
- Long lead times and Online Travel Agents are major contributors to cancellations
- Price (ADR) alone does not strongly influence cancellation behavior
- Longer stay bookings are more reliable and less likely to be canceled
- Clear seasonal trends exist in bookings, pricing, and cancellations

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Apply stricter cancellation policies for long lead-time bookings
- Reduce dependency on Online Travel Agents by promoting direct bookings
- Encourage long-stay bookings through discounts and offers
- Use dynamic pricing strategies during peak seasons
